BTC Historical Collector

Privacy policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026.

Operator and purpose

Ibrahim Rashid operates this personal utility to preserve historical market data in his own Google Drive.

Google data and permissions

The application requests Google Drive access. This permission can cover the account’s Drive files; the collector uses the approved archive destinations. It reads folder and file metadata, sizes and checksums, creates archive folders, uploads market files, and reads archive content for integrity or recovery checks.

Storage and use

Authorization credentials, transfer state and verification manifests are stored locally on the operator’s Mac. Market archives are stored in the operator’s Google Drive. Temporary local market files are removed after successful remote verification and a committed receipt. Credentials are not included in the public information pages.

Sharing and retention

The utility does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. It has no separate application backend receiving archive content or credentials. Google processes data as the Drive and identity provider. The public website host receives ordinary website traffic; these pages contain no analytics or tracking scripts. The operator controls archive retention and account sharing.

Control and contact

The operator can revoke the application’s access in Google Account third-party connections settings and manage stored archives in Drive. Questions: ibrahimrashid5122@gmail.com.

The application’s use of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
