BTC Historical Collector

A personal research utility operated by Ibrahim Rashid to acquire historical cryptocurrency market data and preserve verified archives in his own Google Drive.

The utility runs on the operator’s Mac. It downloads source market files, checks their integrity, uploads them to approved archive folders, verifies the uploaded content, and removes completed temporary staging copies.

Google Drive access is used to find archive folders, create folders, upload market files, read file metadata and checksums, and perform recovery checks. The collector does not automatically delete remote archives.

This application is for the operator’s personal use. Google authorization is required before it can access the account. Access can be revoked through the Google Account’s third-party connections settings.
